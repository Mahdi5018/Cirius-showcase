# Digital marketing measurement plan

Illustrative strategy case study, not a disclosure of actual launch plans, campaigns, budgets or customer results.

## Acquisition and activation

| Funnel stage | Candidate tactic | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Localized content and route-focused education | Qualified reach, CTR |
| Acquisition | Route landing pages and university/community outreach | Cost per qualified signup |
| Activation | Clear onboarding and first search or ride publication | Activated users / signups |
| Conversion | Route availability and useful booking information | Completed first journeys / activated users |
| Retention | Useful saved-route alerts and repeat ride coordination | Repeat completed journey rate |

A two-sided marketplace requires supply and demand on the same routes and dates. Measure active drivers, offered seats, search-to-request conversion and completion together rather than optimizing installs alone.

## Measurement design

Proposed events: signup_completed, search_performed, ride_published, booking_requested, booking_accepted, ride_completed. These are illustrative event names, not a claim of implemented instrumentation. Collect only necessary, approved fields; exclude private messages, precise locations and personal contact details from marketing analytics.

Use UTM-tagged campaign links, cohort-based reporting and clearly defined attribution windows. CAC = attributable acquisition spend / new acquired customers; retention = cohort members with repeat completed journeys / eligible cohort members. Do not interpret synthetic ride data as acquisition attribution.

## Experiment proposal

Compare route-oriented messaging with general convenience messaging, using a preselected primary metric (qualified signup rate) and guardrails (complaints, bounce rate). Define allocation and sample size before launch; report uncertainty and avoid declaring a winner from small samples.
