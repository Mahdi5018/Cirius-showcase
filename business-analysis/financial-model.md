# Financial modelling | Illustrative scenarios

All amounts are TND. This is a teaching model, not Cirius pricing, revenue, expenses or approved monetization policy. Actual payment processing and monetization are not claimed.

## Hypothetical transaction-based scenario

Assume a 15 TND average seat contribution, 10% platform fee, 0.50 TND variable platform cost per completed seat and 1,500 TND monthly fixed cost.

Contribution per seat = 15 × 10% − 0.50 = 1 TND.

| Monthly scenario | Low | Base | High |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Completed seat journeys | 500 | 1,500 | 3,000 |
| Gross seat value | 7,500 | 22,500 | 45,000 |
| Hypothetical platform revenue | 750 | 2,250 | 4,500 |
| Variable cost | 250 | 750 | 1,500 |
| Contribution | 500 | 1,500 | 3,000 |
| Fixed cost | 1,500 | 1,500 | 1,500 |
| Simplified operating result | −1,000 | 0 | 1,500 |

Break-even = fixed cost / positive contribution per seat = 1,500 completed seats/month.

## Fee sensitivity

| Hypothetical fee | Contribution / seat | Break-even seats/month |
|---|---:|---:|
| 5% | 0.25 | 6,000 |
| 10% | 1.00 | 1,500 |
| 15% | 1.75 | 858 |

Round break-even up to whole seats. Taxes, refunds, payment fees beyond the assumed variable cost, insurance, compliance, marketing ramp-up and working capital are excluded. No inference about actual willingness to pay is warranted.

The associated [CSV scenario model](financial-scenarios.csv) is suitable for Excel or Power BI import. Commercial feasibility requires legal review, customer research, cost quotes and validated pilot activity.
