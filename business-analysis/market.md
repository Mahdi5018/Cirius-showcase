# Market intelligence | Tunisia

Research snapshot: 24 November 2026. Public sources support market context, not verified Cirius demand.

## Market context

Tunisia's 2024 population census recorded 11,972,169 residents on 6 November 2024. Population is contextual only; it is not the number of people who need carpooling or are eligible to use it.

### Competitor and substitute scan

| Alternative | Publicly described offer | Research question |
|---|---|---|
| Droovo | Tunisian intercity carpooling and ride sharing | Route coverage, liquidity and repeat use |
| GoVoiturage | Ride search and participation-cost guidance | Booking reliability and user experience |
| Machii | Carpooling introductions; cost sharing arranged between parties | Coordination and payment friction |
| Louage / intercity bus / train | Established shared or scheduled transport alternatives | Total journey cost, time, availability and convenience |

No market shares or competitor user counts have been verified. Website claims are not independent performance evidence.

## Segment hypotheses

- Students: affordability, predictable trips between home and university.
- Working travellers: schedule coordination and reliability.
- Occasional intercity travellers: clarity about pickup, luggage and availability.
- Drivers with spare seats: capacity utilization and cost sharing.

Validate with interviews, route-level surveys and a controlled pilot; do not infer actual behaviour from the personas.

## Illustrative annual market sizing

Bottom-up example (not a forecast or measured market):

| Input | Assumption |
|---|---:|
| Eligible intercity travellers | 500,000 |
| Potential paid/shared seat journeys per traveller annually | 6 |
| Average seat contribution | 15 TND |
| Share within serviceable routes | 30% |
| Illustrative obtainable share of serviceable journeys | 2% |

| Measure | Annual seat journeys | Gross seat contribution value |
|---|---:|---:|
| TAM | 3,000,000 | 45,000,000 TND |
| SAM | 900,000 | 13,500,000 TND |
| SOM | 18,000 | 270,000 TND |

TAM = eligible travellers × journeys; SAM = TAM × serviceable share; SOM = SAM × obtainable share. Gross seat contribution is not platform revenue. Every model input above is hypothetical; no population percentage is represented as a fact. Replace assumptions with measured route demand, active driver supply, eligibility, willingness to pay and pilot conversion before investment decisions.
